About tanstack-fetch ​

tanstack-fetch is a lightweight alternative to axios/ky for apps using @tanstack/react-query. It returns data, throws typed FetchErrors, and honors AbortSignal — matching the Query queryFn mental model.

Created and maintained by Mohammad Garmabi.
